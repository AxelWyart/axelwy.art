I’m Axel, a product design leader with 10+ years building and scaling digital products, most recently in AI-driven platforms.

I care deeply about craft, but I care more about judgment.

The work

My work sits at the intersection of strategy, delivery, and reality: aligning executives, product, and engineering while making sure what we build actually works for users.

Now

Design Lead, Foundever

Leading design for AI solutions used by thousands of agents worldwide, every day.

Previously

Head of Product Design, Laiye

Scaling AI and automation products globally through growth, complexity, and acquisition.

Before that

Building design at Mindsay

Building and scaling the design function from the ground up, laying foundations that supported its acquisition.

How I think

Good design is opinionated, constrained, and accountable.

I ask hard questions early, let go of weak ideas quickly, and help teams move from vision decks to shipped products.

I partner with founders and AI teams across brand, marketing, product, and design operations. The aim is to turn strategy into outcomes that hold up in the real world.

Beyond the day job

When I’m not designing or leading teams, I’m usually refining systems, mentoring designers, or working on personal projects.

Those projects give me room to experiment without permission. I like keeping that space alongside the work of building products and leading teams.
