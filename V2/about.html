Work and life

Axel Wyart

Design Director · Biarritz, France

I design tools for people who have to make decisions quickly. At Foundever, I lead design for AI products used by thousands of customer-service agents. That can mean helping someone find an answer mid-conversation or making a new tool easier to learn on their first shift.

I built the design function at Mindsay, working across its conversational products, workflow builder, and brand. After Laiye acquired Mindsay in 2022, I led product design across a broader automation platform, helping bring navigation, workflows, and embedded assistants together.

I still like getting close to the work. A prototype gives the team something concrete to disagree about, and a good review helps us decide which details matter. I’ve worked that way on everything from a lenticular print to an AI assistant that lives inside someone else’s software.
