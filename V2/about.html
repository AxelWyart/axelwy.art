Life outside work

Close to the craft.
Invested in the team.

I’m Axel, a design director based in Biarritz. I help teams turn complex product problems into experiences people can understand and use.

Today, I lead product design and research at Foundever, across a suite of AI and customer experience products. I set direction across products, help designers grow, and stay close to the details that matter. When someone is helping a customer live on a call, a confusing interface becomes their problem immediately.

Previously, I built the design practice at Mindsay. After the acquisition, I led product design at Laiye. I also co-founded Nudge, an assistant built into the software it helped people learn.

  • ✓Led design for AI tools used by thousands of support agents at Foundever.
  • ✓Built Mindsay’s design practice and helped the team raise a $10M Series A.

I do my best work when I can connect the bigger picture to something tangible. A rough prototype to test an assumption. A clearer interaction to unblock a task. A shared design system that helps a whole team move forward. I still make things myself because it helps me ask better questions and give more useful feedback.

My background spans branding, physical objects, and software. I like moving between disciplines. It keeps me curious about how things work, how they feel, and why people care about them.

Away from work, life is mostly around Biarritz with my wife and our son, Orso. The photos probably tell that part better than another paragraph could.
