Life outside work

Axel Wyart

Design Director · Biarritz, France

I’m Axel, a designer based in Biarritz. I lead design at Foundever, where we build AI tools for customer support. A lot of the work comes down to small moments, like helping an agent find the right answer while someone is still on the line.

Before Foundever, I built the design practice at Mindsay and led product design at Laiye after the acquisition. I also co-founded Nudge, an assistant that lived inside the software it was there to explain.

I’ve worked on brands, physical objects, and software. A lenticular print and an AI assistant have more in common than you’d think. Both depend on noticing what someone does next and getting the response to feel right.

As a design lead, I like staying close enough to the work to try things myself. I’d rather put a rough prototype in front of the team and talk through what feels off than spend another hour describing how it should work.
