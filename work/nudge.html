Nudge

Co-founder

I co-founded Nudge to help people get more from the software they already use. My role combined shaping the product with designing it: turning the idea of an in-product AI assistant into an interface people could open, understand, and use.

I designed the widget’s core experience, from the question-mark launcher to asking a question, waiting for an answer, and continuing the conversation. Keeping the surrounding page visible was central to the design. People needed help with the task in front of them, with a clear way back to their work.

Working closely with Guillaume, Yoann, and Santi, I connected the product experience with how we introduced Nudge. I also designed the waitlist page, bringing the pitch and the widget together so people could understand the idea before signing up.
